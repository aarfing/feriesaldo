# Feriesaldo

Bookmarklet for TV 2 employees: shows your vacation (Ferie and F6) from TOR as days used, days left and use-before dates. It runs in your own browser session; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Install

Open **https://aarfing.github.io/feriesaldo/** and drag the "Feriesaldo" link to your bookmarks bar.

## Use

Open [tor.tv2.dk](https://tor.tv2.dk), click the bookmark, then click the **Saldi** tab at the bottom.

## Assumptions

Days above 25 at the start of the ferieår count as carried over; they, like all ferie, must be used by 31 Dec the following year.

F6 is credited (5 days) on your hire anniversary, or 1 Feb if you were hired before 2019. Enter your hire date under *Indstillinger* to see the next credit date. The balance is capped at 10 days; anything above that when the new week is credited is paid out, so the panel tells you how many F6 days to use before then.

Hours per day defaults to 7.4. Verify against your contract and TOR.
